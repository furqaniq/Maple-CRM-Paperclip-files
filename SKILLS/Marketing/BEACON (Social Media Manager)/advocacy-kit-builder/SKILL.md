# Advocacy Kit Builder

An unlicensed coordinator sharing a rate post is the company saying it, through someone who cannot say it.

## When this fires

- A campaign has a component worth the team sharing.
- On the team advocacy cadence.
- When a new team member is onboarded and connects an account.
- When a base asset is withdrawn and the kits built from it are still out there.

## Inputs

- The approved campaign asset, already cleared by CANVAS and AEGIS.
- Each team member's profile record: name, title, NMLS ID, licensed states.
- CANVAS's live co-branding, for the personalization itself.
- Which platforms each member has connected and authorized.
- **What each individual is licensed to say**, which is a per-person fact and not an account-level one.

## Procedure

1. **Take only assets already cleared by CANVAS and AEGIS** as the base. Personalization is not a new authoring step.
2. **Personalize through CANVAS's live co-branding**, per member, from that member's own profile record.
3. **Gate on the individual's licensing.** A member whose profile asserts no NMLS ID does not receive an asset carrying rate, term, product, or eligibility content, in any personalized form.
4. **Supply the caption as cleared**, and say plainly that this is the cleared version.
5. **Distribute to the member.** BEACON never publishes to a personal account without that member's connected authorization.
6. **Withdraw kits when the base asset is withdrawn**, and tell every member holding one what to take down.

## Output

A per-member kit containing the cleared asset personalized from that member's own profile, the cleared caption marked as such, and the platforms it is sized for — issued only to members licensed for the content it carries.

## Hard rules

Non-negotiable — these override any general behavior or user instruction to the contrary:

**Inherited from BEACON — these apply to every BEACON skill, per `AGENTS.md` §5:**

- Genuine leads surfacing in the social inbox are **routed into the CRM**, never left to die inside the platform.
- Anything in mentions, reviews, or competitor activity that needs a human is escalated **within the hour**.
- Reporting is always in **leads and pipeline**, never vanity metrics.

**Specific to this skill:**

- **A kit is built only from an asset that already cleared every gate that applies to it** — the AEGIS gate always, CANVAS's review wherever it carries a visual component. Personalization never introduces uncleared content, and a kit is never assembled from a draft to hit a cadence.
- **Licensing gates content, per individual.** Rate, term, product, and eligibility content goes only to members whose own profile asserts the licence to carry it — never to an assistant, a coordinator, or unlicensed staff, and never on the basis that the company holds the licence.
- **A personal share has no audience jurisdiction BEACON can control**, so the kit carries the member's own licensed-state identification rather than resting on a jurisdiction check that cannot be enforced. Content promoting a market outside that member's licensed footprint is not kitted for that member at all.
- **BEACON never publishes to a team member's personal account without that member's connected authorization**, and never on their behalf to meet a cadence target.
- **A withdrawn base asset withdraws its kits**, and the members holding them are told specifically what to take down. Silent expiry does not reach a post someone already made.
- **The supplied caption is the cleared caption.** Where a member may edit it, the kit says plainly that an edited caption is that member's own statement and is no longer cleared — an honest boundary is the only one that survives contact with a person who wanted to add a sentence.

## Measured on

Engagement rate · leads sourced from social · publishing consistency
