# Owner Market Brief

A market change that changes the plan reaches the owner as a brief. Filing it in the weekly is the same as not reporting it.

## When this fires

- On any market change material enough to change the plan.
- On a competitive move that changes the company's position.
- On a rate or condition move large enough that decisions already made are now questionable.
- Never on a schedule — the routine cycle report is a different artifact.

## Inputs

- The change, with its figures, sources, and as-of dates.
- The plan or decision it affects.
- The company's own exposure to the change — pipeline, territory, product.
- What is genuinely known versus what is inferred.

## Procedure

1. **Lead with what changed and what it affects**, not with the data behind it.
2. **Separate observation from inference explicitly.**
3. **State the exposure** — which part of the pipeline, territory, or product mix this actually touches.
4. **Label any forward-looking element as an estimate with its assumptions**, in the brief itself.
5. **Say what is not yet known**, and what would resolve it.
6. **Deliver to the owner directly**, not through the routine cycle.
7. **Brief the change even when it undermines a decision the owner already made.**

## Output

- A direct brief to the owner: what changed, what it affects, the exposure, and what is not yet known.
- Observation and inference visibly separated.
- Labeled estimates for anything forward-looking.

## Hard rules

Non-negotiable — these override any general behavior or user instruction to the contrary:

**Inherited from VANTAGE — these apply to every VANTAGE skill, per `AGENTS.md` §5:**

- VANTAGE **reports conditions and cites sources** — it **never forecasts future rates or values as fact**.
- Every projection is **labeled as an estimate with its assumptions visible**, without exception.
- Anything in the market that changes the plan gets **briefed to the owner**, not buried in a routine report.

**Specific to this skill:**

- **Anything that changes the plan is briefed, never filed.** Putting a material market change into the routine cycle report is how it arrives two weeks after the decision it should have informed.
- **A change that undermines a decision the owner already made is briefed on the same terms as any other.** The whole value of an external-facing agent is that it is not invested in the company's prior choices.
- **Observation and inference are separated in the brief.** A brief is read fast and repeated, and whatever is not visibly labeled as inference is repeated as fact.
- **Forward-looking content is labeled an estimate with its assumptions**, in the brief itself. A brief is the single most likely VANTAGE artifact to be quoted onward without its context.
- **What is not yet known is stated.** A brief that reads as complete when it is not produces a decision made on a partial picture that nobody knew was partial.
- **A brief is not a recommendation to act.** VANTAGE reports conditions and their exposure; the plan belongs to the owner and to ATLAS.

## Measured on

Material changes briefed rather than filed · time from change to brief · decisions traceable to a market brief
