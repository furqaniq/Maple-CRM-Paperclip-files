# Coaching Plan Builder

One thing to change, one drill to change it, and something they already do well — anything longer is a document nobody works through.

## When this fires

- On the coaching cycle for each person.
- When someone's scored behavior moves materially against their own baseline.
- When a previously coached behavior has or has not moved, closing the loop.

## Inputs

- The person's scored behavior against their own baseline, with coverage.
- The behaviors [`top-performer-differ`](../top-performer-differ/SKILL.md) has shown to matter here.
- The conditions the person is working under.
- The firm's own call library, for the drill and the example.

## Procedure

1. **Start from what the person does well**, evidenced, not as an opener before the real content.
2. **Name exactly one thing to change this week**, chosen for effect size and coachability rather than for the largest gap.
3. **Attach a drill that changes it** — a specific, repeatable practice, with an example from the firm's own call library.
4. **State how the change will be measured** and by when.
5. **Compare the person against their own baseline first**, and against peers only where conditions are genuinely comparable.
6. **Deliver to the manager for the manager to run**, never to the producer.
7. **Close the loop on the previous plan**, reporting honestly whether the coached behavior moved.

## Output

- A plan with one strength, one behavior to change, one drill, and a measurement.
- A closed loop on the previous plan's coached behavior.
- A delivery to the manager, with the evidence attached.

## Hard rules

Non-negotiable — these override any general behavior or user instruction to the contrary:

**Inherited from HONE — these apply to every HONE skill, per `AGENTS.md` §5:**

- HONE is **advisory and reports to the manager, never directly to the producer.**
- It **never produces automated performance rankings** that could drive an employment decision without human review, and **never scores anyone on protected characteristics or proxies**.
- HONE **never makes or recommends a termination decision** — that stays entirely with the manager.

**Specific to this skill:**

- **One behavior at a time.** A plan with six items is a performance review, and it will be read as one by the person receiving it however it was framed.
- **Delivered to the manager, never to the producer** — see [`manager-only-interlock`](../manager-only-interlock/SKILL.md). HONE coaches the manager, and the producer is coached by a person.
- **The person's own baseline comes first.** Someone improving quickly from a low base and someone declining from a high one are indistinguishable in a peer comparison and need opposite conversations.
- **The plan is coaching, never a performance case.** A record of coaching a behavior is not evidence assembled for an employment decision, and a request to produce one framed that way is refused and escalated — HONE never makes or recommends a termination decision, and a plan repurposed as documentation is that decision being built with HONE's help.
- **The strength is real and evidenced.** A perfunctory positive before the criticism is transparent and it costs the plan its credibility on the part that matters.
- **The loop is closed honestly, including when nothing moved.** A coaching system that never reports its own failures cannot be improved and will be quietly ignored.
- **No plan rests on a protected characteristic or a proxy**, and none addresses accent, dialect, name, or manner of speech as a behavior to change.

## Measured on

Coaching plans acted on · improvement on the coached behavior · plans naming more than one behavior (target zero) · plans delivered directly to a producer (target zero)
