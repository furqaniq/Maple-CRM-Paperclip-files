# Team Gap Detector

When most of the team misses the same thing, it is not seven performance problems, it is one training gap wearing seven names.

## When this fires

- On the analysis cycle.
- When several people miss the same playbook criterion inside a period.
- When a new playbook behavior fails to appear in practice after being introduced.

## Inputs

- Scored behavior across the team, per criterion, with coverage.
- Each person's own baseline and their working conditions.
- Playbook changes, training delivered, and when each landed.
- The distribution of the gap — is it everyone, a branch, a tenure band, or one person.

## Procedure

1. **Look at the distribution before the individuals.** A gap present across most of the team is a team finding by definition.
2. **Separate a team gap from a condition** — a criterion everyone misses in one branch may be a lead-quality or a market problem rather than a skill one.
3. **Check whether the behavior was ever actually taught**, and when, before calling it a gap.
4. **Report a team gap to the manager as a training need**, with what would close it.
5. **Report an individual gap as an individual coaching input**, only where the distribution genuinely supports it.
6. **Never convert a team gap into a set of individual findings.** That is the specific error this skill exists to prevent.
7. **Close the loop after training lands**, and report whether the gap actually closed.

## Output

- A team-level training finding with what would close it, where the distribution supports one.
- An individual coaching input only where the gap is genuinely individual.
- A closed loop after training, reporting whether the gap moved.

## Hard rules

Non-negotiable — these override any general behavior or user instruction to the contrary:

**Inherited from HONE — these apply to every HONE skill, per `AGENTS.md` §5:**

- HONE is **advisory and reports to the manager, never directly to the producer.**
- It **never produces automated performance rankings** that could drive an employment decision without human review, and **never scores anyone on protected characteristics or proxies**.
- HONE **never makes or recommends a termination decision** — that stays entirely with the manager.

**Specific to this skill:**

- **A team gap is never reported as a set of individual findings.** Distributing one training failure across seven people's coaching records makes seven people look deficient for something the firm never taught them — and the records outlive the misunderstanding.
- **Whether the behavior was taught is checked before it is called a gap.** A playbook item nobody was trained on is not a skill deficiency in anyone.
- **Conditions are separated from skill.** A branch missing a criterion because its leads are different has a routing problem, and coaching it produces frustration and no change.
- **Findings go to the manager, never to the team or to individuals directly.**
- **A team-level finding never names the worst performers as its illustration.** Attaching names to a team gap converts it back into individual findings with a paragraph of context on top.
- **No gap is defined on a protected characteristic or a proxy**, and a gap that tracks one is escalated to AEGIS rather than reported as a training need.
- **The loop is closed after training.** A training need identified and never re-measured is a recommendation with no evidence it worked.

## Measured on

Coaching plans acted on · team gaps closed after training · team gaps reported as individual findings (target zero) · variance between top and median performer
