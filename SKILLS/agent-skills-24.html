How to read this. A skill here is a unit of work you could scope, build, and test on its own — the thing you would hand to an engineer or define as one tool in an agent’s toolset. It is deliberately not a restatement of the handbook’s responsibility bullets. A skill marked interlock implements that agent’s named Hard boundary from the handbook: it exists because a regulator, not a product manager, requires it, and it cannot be configured off. Eleven of the twenty-four agents carry one; the remaining thirteen have no boundary the handbook states as absolute, and are unmarked here rather than assigned a boundary the source does not give them.
